FAQ

Where is my data stored? On your phone, and nowhere else. SetBuild has no server and no account; nothing you record or write ever leaves your device.

I want my data gone. "Delete all my data" at the bottom of the Settings tab permanently removes everything the app has stored on your device.

I reinstalled the app and my old sets are gone. That's expected: everything is stored only on your device, so deleting or reinstalling the app removes it. There is no account to recover it from.
