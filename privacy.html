Privacy

SetBuild does not collect, store, or send your data anywhere. There is no server, no account, and no login.

Everything you write or record stays on your device, in the app's own private storage:

  • Your plans (the bits, last lines, and notes you write).
  • Your recordings.
  • Your ratings and notes.

None of it is sent to us or to anyone else. There is no analytics, no advertising, and nothing that tracks you.

"Delete all my data" (bottom of the Settings tab) permanently removes everything the app has stored on your device. There is nothing anywhere else to delete, since nothing ever left your device in the first place.

Deleting or reinstalling the app also removes everything, the same way removing any app does, since nothing is backed up anywhere by SetBuild itself.
